Overview

Personal audio

Build your sound library on your Windows PC, then trigger sounds from your desktop or phone. SimplySound routes effects to the output you choose, with optional local monitoring when you want to hear them too.

Sounds stay in your setup. Your phone connects to the soundboard over your private Wi-Fi network, or through Tailscale when you’re away. There’s no cloud library to set up.

Made for Windows 10 & 11License MITCurrent release 1.0.2 · Early Beta

Features

At a glance
  • Quick sound triggersReplay or retrigger clips without losing the moment.
  • Phone controlOpen the same soundboard on your LAN or private Tailscale network.
  • Your library, your layoutImport multiple files, add artwork, and arrange your buttons.
  • Audio controlsPick your output devices, tune each sound, and use global hotkeys.

Getting started

Three quick steps
  1. 1
    Install or use the portable app

    Choose the installer or portable Windows download from Releases.

  2. 2
    Add your sounds

    Import audio files in the desktop app and choose your output device.

  3. 3
    Connect your phone

    Use the LAN link or QR code shown in the app. Tailscale is optional for remote access.
